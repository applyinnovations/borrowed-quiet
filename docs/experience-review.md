# Autonomous experience assessment

This is an **AI assessment of the inspected experience**, not measured fear,
enjoyment or paranoia in human players. It combines the independent blind visual
review, complete audio-input reviews, actual survival input/state assertions and
the natural recording's event log. The complete declared work is a restrained
ambient horror layer, not a pursuit game or content-heavy campaign.

## Observable experience

In the reviewed natural session, the player repeatedly mines a workpiece and walks
around an unfinished wooden structure and stone wall in normally generated terrain.
The test restores only its workpiece/position and never forces an episode, changes
the mod clock or resets the director. This limited work pattern is not presented
as unrestricted human exploration. The separate three-seed ordinary-play suite
also checks placement, planting/harvest, crafting, combat and movement outcomes.

## Rubric decisions and rationale

- **Atmosphere and anticipation:** the strongest inspected moment is the shoreline
  form embedded in work, not the static showcase. A scrape and later partial view
  supply a possible interpretation of the earlier material noises. Predicted dread
  comes from interpreting an otherwise ordinary space, not from measured fear.
- **Perceptibility and ambiguity:** quiet-context impacts/rustles and directional
  subtitles are identifiable. Rain can mask subtle sound and the abstract shape
  can resemble a bent plank. These are genuine limits. Neither a recognizable
  creature nor guaranteed notice was promised; stronger volume would undermine
  the chosen restraint and player settings.
- **Coherence:** pace follows stopping, wall taps follow work, and the Fold shares
  the dry material vocabulary. Controlled eligibility/non-activation tests and
  natural context together support this relation; merely scheduling random noise
  would not. The original shape contracts consistently, without missing-resource
  visuals, forced camera movement or fake system failure.
- **Pacing and recovery:** minutes of quiet surround seconds of activity. This
  implements contrast motivated by enjoyable-fear research without claiming its
  population findings determine an optimal Minecraft interval.
- **Repetition:** the test-room four-pulse loops reveal the small vocabulary, as
  intended for inspection. Delivered events vary position, timing, variants and
  turn response. Familiarity remains possible; the project does not claim infinite
  novelty or procedural new assets.
- **Fairness and agency:** no episode damages, moves, penalizes or blocks the
  player. Actual mining resumes during/after the shoreline appearance. The two
  pace recognition endings and forced visual contraction cases show responsive
  termination. Reliable off, reduced motion, visual suppression, mod gain and
  vanilla audio controls have actual runtime tests.
- **Ordinary play:** three-seed resulting-state assertions and the extended work
  recording demonstrate continued work. They do not establish every possible
  player's reaction, mod-pack compatibility or every untested machine's performance.

**Experiential decision: accepted for the committed scope.** The subsequent
[four-hour comparison and repeat-exposure review](endurance-review.md) also passed
the separate technical endurance/performance gates. No human testimony or synthetic
rating is used. Technical acceptance is recorded separately in the final dossier.
