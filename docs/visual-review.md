## Independent blind review

A separate agent received only the completed recordings, without specifications,
event logs or explanations. It sampled the complete 31:09.735 natural recording
every five seconds and the complete 02:39.400 presentation every second, then
examined selected changes at 4–5 fps. It explicitly did **not** listen to audio;
subtitles below are visual evidence. Events between overview samples may be missed.

In natural play, it observed repeated walking and stone mining through day, night,
sunrise and rain. At 07:31–07:39, directional knock subtitles appear while walking
continues. Footfall subtitles occur near 10:27.5 and 27:27.5. At 14:54–14:56, a
scraping subtitle appears facing the stone wall. At 14:57–14:59.7, a thin bent black
form is visible near the shoreline, left of the crosshair. By 15:00–15:02, the
camera has turned and the player is again breaking stone and producing drops.
The primary agent independently inspected that native 14:57.5 frame and the dense
14:54–15:02 sequence. Continued work is visible; no human reaction is inferred.

The reviewer found no visible damage, inventory clearing, persistent overlay or
sustained work obstruction. It judged the 14:57.5 contextual encounter the most
persuasive evidence, and the presentation's 02:34.5 corridor the strongest visual
atmosphere. Its important criticism remains recorded: the simple shape can read
as a bent plank, ribbon or unfinished geometry, and the 02:26.0 close night view
has weak material/grounding cues. Frequent showcase repetitions make the pattern
easy to learn; the natural session does not use that frequency.

## Design decision

**Visual asset accepted.** This is an AI aesthetic assessment, not measured player
fear. The regular, smooth contraction and absence of rendering defects make the
shape's impossible behaviour intentional in the inspected natural context. Its
unresolved identity serves the committed premise; adding a face or detailed body
would change that premise. The daylight showcase's weak atmosphere is not used as
proof of fear. Narrow geometry, minimal surface detail and subtle grounding are
documented aesthetic limitations, not missing promised textures or animations.
